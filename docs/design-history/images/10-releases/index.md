# Release screenshots, v1.0.0 to v6.0.0

[All images](../README.md) · [Narrative](../../01-starting-point.md)

Every shipped version from 2019 to Letterpress, rebuilt from its commit with the
toolchain of its era and captured at 1440px desktop and 390px phone width, in
dark and light where the version had both. v6.0.0 was captured from production.
The same files are attached to the
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/AMEH64/allenhansen.dev/releases); they're
repeated here so the history is complete in one place.
